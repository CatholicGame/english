# Chủ đề 1: Collocations & Phrasal Verbs — Động từ cơ bản

> Tài liệu nội dung (content spec) cho chủ đề đầu tiên của app. Mỗi mục từ vựng có cấu trúc chuẩn để sau này chuyển sang JSON/DB: `term`, `type` (collocation | phrasal_verb | prepositional_verb), `definition_en`, `definition_vi`, `example`. Ngôn ngữ hiển thị mặc định là **tiếng Anh**, có thể chuyển sang tiếng Việt.

## Mục lục động từ cơ bản

| Nhóm (Group) | Mô tả | Động từ |
|---|---|---|
| A. Action / Creation | Hành động tạo ra, thực hiện, sở hữu | DO, MAKE, TAKE, GIVE, PUT, HAVE, PAY, THROW |
| B. Movement | Di chuyển, mang/đưa | GO, COME, BRING, CARRY, RUN, MOVE, DROP, PULL, PUSH, PASS, FALL |
| C. Hold / Change / State | Nắm giữ, thay đổi trạng thái | GET, KEEP, HOLD, BREAK, TURN, CUT, STAND |
| D. Other core verbs | Các động từ đa dụng, giao tiếp | CALL, LOOK, PICK, SET, FIND, WORK, CHECK, FILL, HAND, POINT, DEAL |
| E. Prepositional verbs | Verb + giới từ cố định (thường 1 nghĩa) | CARE, DEPEND, FOCUS, LISTEN, BELONG, APPLY, REFER, RESULT, LEAD, CONSIST, SUFFER, RECOVER |

Tổng cộng: **49 động từ cơ bản**.

### 35. HAND
**Classification:** D. Other core verbs
**Definition (EN):** to give something to someone using your hand
**Định nghĩa (VI):** trao, đưa bằng tay

#### Collocations
| Collocation | EN Meaning | VI Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| hand-picked | carefully chosen | được chọn lựa kỹ càng | These are hand-picked employees for the project. |
| hand in hand | closely connected, together | đi đôi với nhau | Success and hard work go hand in hand. |
| hand-to-hand | close combat fought directly with physical force, not weapons | giáp lá cà, chiến đấu tay đôi | The soldiers were trained for hand-to-hand combat. |
| first-hand | gained through direct personal experience, not from others | trực tiếp, tận mắt trải nghiệm | She has first-hand experience with the refugee crisis. |
| hands-on | actively involved and practical, rather than just theoretical | thực hành trực tiếp, nhúng tay vào | The workshop is very hands-on, not just lectures. |
| hand in glove | working extremely closely with someone, often secretly | phối hợp ăn ý, đôi khi ám chỉ thông đồng | The two companies work hand in glove on pricing. |
| at hand | nearby and ready to be used when needed | sẵn có, trong tầm tay | Keep a first-aid kit at hand during the trip. |
| the upper hand | an advantage that lets you control a situation | thế thượng phong, ưu thế hơn | Negotiators gained the upper hand after the leak. |

#### Phrasal Verbs
| Phrasal Verb | EN Meaning | VI Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| hand in | to submit something (homework, resignation) | nộp (bài, đơn) | Please hand in your assignments by Friday. |
| hand out | to distribute something to a group | phát ra, phân phát | The teacher handed out the test papers. |
| hand over | to give control or possession of something to someone | giao lại, chuyển giao quyền | He handed over the keys to the new owner. |
| hand down | to pass something from one generation or authority to the next | truyền lại cho thế hệ sau; tuyên án | The recipe was handed down from her grandmother. |
| hand back | to return something to the person who gave it | trả lại cho ai đó | The teacher handed back our graded essays. |

### 36. POINT
**Classification:** D. Other core verbs
**Definition (EN):** to indicate direction or something specific, often with your finger
**Định nghĩa (VI):** chỉ (tay), chỉ ra, chỉ hướng

#### Collocations
| Collocation | EN Meaning | VI Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| point blank | said or done directly and bluntly | thẳng thừng, trực tiếp | She asked him point blank if he was lying. |
| point of view | a particular way of considering something | quan điểm | I understand your point of view. |
| point of no return | the stage after which it's impossible to stop or turn back | điểm không thể quay đầu | The negotiations passed the point of no return. |
| point in time | a specific moment or stage in a process | thời điểm cụ thể | At this point in time, we can't confirm the launch date. |
| point of contact | the person you should communicate with about something | đầu mối liên hệ | She's our main point of contact for the client. |
| point of interest | a notable place or feature worth visiting or noticing | địa điểm hoặc nét đáng chú ý | The old lighthouse is a popular point of interest. |
| point of sale | the place or moment where a retail purchase happens | điểm bán hàng, thời điểm giao dịch | The discount is applied automatically at the point of sale. |
| point the finger at | to blame someone for something that went wrong | đổ lỗi cho ai đó | Don't point the finger at me, it wasn't my fault. |

#### Phrasal Verbs
| Phrasal Verb | EN Meaning | VI Meaning | Example |

| point out | to indicate or mention something specific | chỉ ra, nêu ra | She pointed out a mistake in the report. |
| point to | to indicate or suggest something | cho thấy, gợi ý về | The evidence points to his involvement. |
| point at | to aim a finger or object directly at someone or something | chỉ thẳng vào ai hoặc cái gì | It's rude to point at people. |
| point up | to emphasize or draw attention to something important | nhấn mạnh, làm nổi bật | The report points up the need for better funding. |
| point toward | to suggest or indicate something, often based on evidence | cho thấy, chỉ ra dựa trên bằng chứng | All the evidence points toward an inside job. |

### 37. DEAL
**Classification:** D. Other core verbs
**Definition (EN):** to handle or take action regarding something
**Định nghĩa (VI):** xử lý, giải quyết

#### Collocations
| Collocation | EN Meaning | VI Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| deal a blow | to cause harm or a serious setback to something | giáng một đòn, gây tổn hại | The lawsuit dealt a blow to the company's reputation. |
| big deal | something considered important or impressive, often said sarcastically | chuyện lớn, chuyện quan trọng, đôi khi mỉa mai | So you won once, big deal. |
| deal drugs | to sell illegal drugs | buôn bán ma túy | He was arrested for dealing drugs near the school. |
| seal the deal | to finalize an agreement or negotiation successfully | chốt thỏa thuận, hoàn tất giao dịch | A handshake sealed the deal between the two companies. |

| strike a deal | to reach an agreement, often after negotiation | đạt được thỏa thuận sau khi thương lượng | The two countries struck a deal on trade tariffs. |

| the real deal | someone or something genuine and as good as claimed | hàng thật, đúng như lời đồn | After tasting the food, we knew this restaurant was the real deal. |

#### Phrasal Verbs

|---|---|---|---|

| deal in | to buy and sell a particular type of goods | buôn bán loại hàng cụ thể | The shop deals in vintage furniture. |

## Nhóm E — Prepositional verbs (verb + giới từ cố định)

Các động từ dưới đây thường chỉ đi với **một giới từ cố định** và mang **một nghĩa chính**, khác với phrasal verbs đa nghĩa ở trên. Phù hợp để dạy như "collocation ngữ pháp" (grammatical collocation).

| Verb + Preposition | EN Meaning | VI Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| care about | to feel concern or interest in something | quan tâm đến | She cares about the environment deeply. |
| care for | to look after someone; to like (formal) | chăm sóc; thích (trang trọng) | He cares for his elderly parents every day. |
| depend on | to rely on someone or something | phụ thuộc vào | Our trip depends on the weather. |
| focus on | to concentrate on something | tập trung vào | Let's focus on the most important task first. |
| listen to | to pay attention to sound or someone speaking | lắng nghe | I like to listen to music while studying. |
| belong to | to be a member of, or owned by, something | thuộc về | This book belongs to the school library. |
| apply for | to formally request something (a job, visa, etc.) | nộp đơn xin | She applied for a scholarship abroad. |
| apply to | to be relevant or applicable to someone/something | áp dụng cho, liên quan đến | This rule applies to all employees. |
| refer to | to mention or direct attention to something | đề cập đến, tham khảo | Please refer to page 10 for more details. |
| result in | to cause a particular outcome | dẫn đến (kết quả) | The negotiation resulted in a new agreement. |
| result from | to be caused by something | là kết quả của, bắt nguồn từ | The delay resulted from bad weather. |
| lead to | to cause something to happen | dẫn đến | Smoking can lead to serious health problems. |
| consist of | to be made up of particular parts | bao gồm, gồm có | The committee consists of five members. |
| suffer from | to be affected by an illness or problem | mắc phải, chịu đựng | He suffers from chronic back pain. |
| recover from | to get better after an illness or difficulty | hồi phục sau | It took months to recover from the surgery. |
